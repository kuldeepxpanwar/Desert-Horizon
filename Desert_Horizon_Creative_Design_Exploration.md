# Animation Ideas

Keep lightweight.

Possible animations: - Hero fade - Text reveal - Card lift - Image
zoom - Counter - Navbar blur - Smooth anchor scrolling - Button ripple -
Review slider

Avoid: - Heavy 3D - Long intros - Auto music - Excessive particles
