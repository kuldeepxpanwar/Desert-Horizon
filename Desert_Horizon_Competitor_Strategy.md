# Desert Horizon -- Competitor Research & Brand Strategy

Version: 1.0

> Purpose: This document analyzes the Jaisalmer Desert Safari market and
> defines how Desert Horizon should differentiate itself. This is a
> strategy document only and does not replace the main PRD.

# Market Overview

The Jaisalmer tourism market is highly competitive. Most operators offer
nearly identical services with similar layouts and marketing messages.

Common services: - Camel Safari - Jeep Safari - Luxury Desert Camp -
Folk Dance - Fire Show - Bonfire - Buffet Dinner - Breakfast - Pickup &
Drop - Quad Bike - Parasailing - Private Events

Customers generally search: - Best Desert Camp Jaisalmer - Desert Safari
Jaisalmer - Sam Sand Dunes - Luxury Camp Jaisalmer - Jeep Safari
Jaisalmer - Camel Safari Jaisalmer

# Competitor Patterns

Most competitors use: - Generic logos - Generic templates - Similar
package descriptions - Large text blocks - Weak branding - Poor
storytelling - Old UI - Limited trust signals

Typical package includes: - Welcome drink - Camel ride - Jeep safari -
Tea & snacks - Buffet dinner - Cultural programme - Folk dance - Fire
show - Bonfire - Luxury tent stay - Breakfast

# Opportunities

Instead of selling packages, Desert Horizon should sell experiences.

Replace generic wording with emotional storytelling.

Example:

Instead of: "Luxury Camp"

Use: "Spend an unforgettable night beneath the stars of the Thar
Desert."

# Brand Position

Desert Horizon is NOT just a camp.

It is a premium desert experience brand.

Future services: - Luxury Camps - Desert Safari - Jeep Safari - Camel
Safari - Taxi - Hotels - Custom Tours - Corporate Events - Destination
Weddings
